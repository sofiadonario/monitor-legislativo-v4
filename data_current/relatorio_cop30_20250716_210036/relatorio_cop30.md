# Brazil's Transport Policy Evolution: Evidence for COP 30

## Executive Summary

Brazil has significantly intensified its regulatory framework for sustainable transport following the Paris Agreement (2015), demonstrating concrete commitment to climate goals through policy action.

## Key Findings

### 1. Post-Paris Acceleration
- **1089% increase** in regulatory activity post-2015
- **365 new regulations** since Paris Agreement
- Clear policy response to international climate commitments

### 2. Institutional Framework
- Multi-level governance approach with federal coordination
- Strong role of regulatory agencies (ANEEL, ANP, ANTT)
- Integration of environmental and transport policies

### 3. Policy Instruments
Brazil employs diverse policy instruments:
- **Doutrina**: 51.9% of total framework
- **Legislation**: 24.7% of total framework
- **Other**: 17.3% of total framework
- **Jurisprudence**: 5.9% of total framework
- **Filtros**: 0.1% of total framework

### 4. Recent Milestones
- **2017**: RenovaBio Program (Biofuels Policy)
- **2022**: Future Fuels Framework
- **2023**: Enhanced coordination mechanisms

## Brazil's Commitment to COP 30

This data demonstrates Brazil's proactive approach to transport decarbonization, positioning the country as a leader in sustainable transport policies for the Amazon COP. The regulatory framework provides a solid foundation for achieving NDC targets and showcasing Brazilian innovation in climate policy.
